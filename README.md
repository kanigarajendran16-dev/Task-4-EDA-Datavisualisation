# Data Visualization and Storytelling

This project focuses on analyzing a sales dataset and presenting meaningful business insights through data visualization.

## Objective
To identify trends, patterns, and business insights from structured sales data and convert them into a clear visual story.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Overall Sales and Profit Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales

## Key Insights
The analysis helps understand sales trends, identify high-performing categories and regions, and find products that contribute significantly to overall sales.

## Conclusion
Data visualization makes complex sales data easier to understand and helps communicate useful business insights for better decision-making.
